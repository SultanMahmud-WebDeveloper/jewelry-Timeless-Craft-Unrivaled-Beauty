About Us

A small atelier devoted to the slow, exacting work of making beautiful things last.

The Maison

Made slowly, made to last

Maison Aurelia began in a single bright workshop with one bench, one lamp and a belief that luxury is a matter of restraint.

Our watchmakers, goldsmiths and leather artisans work side by side, sharing materials, ideas and a standard that no schedule is allowed to lower. Each ring, watch and strap is designed with its owner in mind and finished by hand, in small numbers.

We prefer quiet details to loud ones: a perfectly even polish, a crown that turns with the right resistance, a stitch that disappears into the leather. These are the things that you notice only after years of wearing a piece, and they are the ones we care about most.

— The Aurelia Atelier

Craftsmanship

The Details That Define Us

01

Materials

Ethically sourced gold and platinum, sapphire crystal, conflict-free stones and full-grain leathers from heritage tanneries, chosen for how well they age.

02

Precision

Movements are assembled and regulated by hand, then tested in multiple positions over several weeks. Tolerances are measured in microns, not millimetres.

03

Bespoke Design

Select your metal, dial, stone or leather and we will draw, prototype and finish a piece that is entirely yours, with a signed certificate of origin.
